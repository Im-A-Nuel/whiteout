## 3. Skrip video 3 menit (English)

Rekam sendiri dengan suara lo. Jangan baca naskah datar, latih dua kali. Layar penuh, resolusi 1080p, tutup tab yang tidak perlu. Siapkan wallet demo yang sudah berisi ETH Sepolia.

**0:00 to 0:20, Hook**
> "On a normal blockchain you can't build this game. Everything is public: where the traps are, where you step, even the moment you die. Whiteout is a blizzard roguelike where the chain can't see you die."

*Visual: title card, then the empty snowfield board.*

**0:20 to 0:50, Rules in ten seconds**
> "You start in the cabin. The map is hidden, generated by Zama's onchain encrypted randomness. Each step gives you two private signals: Cold, a pit is next to you, and Glint, treasure is next to you. Bring treasure home before the storm ends, or you score nothing."

*Visual: point at the signal panel and the step counter.*

**0:50 to 2:00, Live run**
> "Watch the left panel: this is what the chain sees."

*Visual: split screen, Chain view on the left (opaque handles, identical transactions), Your view on the right.*
- Take four to six steps. React out loud to the signals ("Cold here, so I'm not going east").
- Call out once: "Every step is an encrypted input. An observer can't tell a move from a bump, and can't tell if I'm alive."
- Pick up one treasure. Then walk back to the cabin and press Finish.
- If the run is long, cut the waiting with a visible timer, never with fake speed-ups.

**2:00 to 2:30, The reveal**
> "Now the fog lifts. The whole map, my exact path and my score are decrypted with a KMS proof. Anyone can replay this run and check that the game was fair."

*Visual: fog lifting, full map, replay animation, score stamp, then the Etherscan transaction for finalize.*

**2:30 to 2:50, How it works**
> "The map, my position and my path are 64-bit encrypted bitboards. A move is just shifts and masks under FHE, about one point eight million homomorphic complexity units per step. Only I can decrypt my own position and signals. The map stays sealed until Finish."

*Visual: a clean slide of the data flow from `ARCHITECTURE.md`, or a quick code scroll.*

**2:50 to 3:00, Close**
> "If the state were public, there would be no game. Whiteout, a game that couldn't exist before. Built on Zama."

*Visual: repo URL, contract address, demo URL.*

Catatan produksi:
- Rekam beberapa take dari run yang sudah dilatih. Pilih satu run yang rapi, jangan mengandalkan keberuntungan satu kali jalan.
- Kalau ada jeda decrypt, isi dengan narasi, bukan keheningan.
- Pastikan wajah atau suara jelas asli. Tidak ada suara sintetis.
- Jangan menyebut angka yang belum lo ukur. Ganti "one point eight million" dengan angka nyata dari hasil ukur.

## 6. Pertanyaan juri yang mungkin muncul (siapkan jawaban)

| Pertanyaan | Jawaban singkat |
| --- | --- |
| Kenapa tidak commit-reveal saja? | Commit-reveal harus membuka rahasia setelah tiap aksi. Di sini peta dan jalur tetap tertutup selama run, dan kematian tidak pernah jadi sinyal publik |
| Bagaimana tahu peta tidak dicurangi? | Peta dibuat oleh RNG terenkripsi onchain sebelum langkah pertama, dibuka penuh dengan proof saat Finish, dan run bisa di-replay |
| Apa yang publik? | Pemilik run, jumlah langkah, dan hasil akhir setelah Finish |
| Kenapa tidak ada lawan? | Solo membuat game bisa dinilai tanpa menunggu pemain lain, dan leaderboard tetap kompetitif |
| Berapa beban komputasi per langkah? | Satu langkah sekitar 1,78 juta HCU hasil hitung dari tabel docs (ganti dengan angka ukur), jauh di bawah limit per transaksi |
